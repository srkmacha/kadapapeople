# KADAPA PEOPLE — DATA COLLECTION SHEET

This document is the specification for the information we collect.

The final data should be entered through the Kadapa People Admin Panel,
not maintained as a separate master Excel file.

## FIRST CATEGORY

Local Businesses

## REQUIRED INFORMATION

### Business identity
- Business name
- Category
- Subcategory
- Description

### Contact
- Phone
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Website

### Location
- Full address
- Locality / area
- Pincode
- Latitude
- Longitude

### Operations
- Opening hours
- Services offered

### Media
- Logo
- Cover image
- Business photos

### Publishing
- Draft / Published / Archived

### Verification
- Unverified / Pending / Verified

### SEO
- SEO title
- SEO description
- URL slug

## OPTIONAL INFORMATION

- Tags
- Special services
- Additional contact information
- Additional location details

## DATA QUALITY

Before publishing a business, check:

[ ] Correct business name
[ ] Correct category
[ ] Correct locality
[ ] Correct address
[ ] Contact information checked
[ ] Opening hours checked where possible
[ ] Location checked where possible
[ ] Photos are appropriate
[ ] No duplicate listing
[ ] Verification status recorded

## DO NOT INVENT DATA

If information is unknown:

Leave it empty.

Do not create:

- fake phone numbers
- fake addresses
- fake opening hours
- fake reviews
- fake business descriptions
- fake photographs

## WHY WE COLLECT THIS

The same business record will eventually power:

- Website business page
- Google/search visibility
- Category pages
- Locality pages
- Search
- Nearby discovery
- Mobile application
- Business owner dashboard
- Reviews
- Enquiries
- Future offers/bookings

ONE BUSINESS ENTRY SHOULD POWER MULTIPLE PRODUCTS.

## FUTURE DATA

Do not collect large amounts of future-category data yet.

Future modules will be introduced separately:

- Education
- Jobs
- Housing
- Events
- Local news
- Government services
- Emergency information
- Community
- Tourism

Each module will get its own proper data model.
